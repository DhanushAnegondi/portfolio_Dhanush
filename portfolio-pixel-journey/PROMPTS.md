# Production Prompt

## Master Direction

```text
Create a cohesive retro pixel-art asset collection for a dark, modern
portfolio website. The assets tell one continuous educational journey:

1. A young university student walks forward carrying a backpack.
2. The student pauses at the MS in the US milestone.
3. A small passenger airplane flies across the scene.
4. An Indiana University milestone appears.
5. The same character transforms into a confident graduate wearing a cap
   and gown and holding a diploma.

The character is a friendly young South Asian male with dark hair, warm
medium-brown skin, a teal jacket, dark trousers, warm-white sneakers, and
an amber backpack. Preserve identity, proportions, clothing, colors, and
silhouette across every frame.

Use authentic polished 16-bit pixel art with deliberate square pixels,
crisp edges, limited color clusters, readable silhouettes, subtle shading,
and no anti-aliasing. Keep the tone professional and mature.

Palette:
- Near-black #080A0D
- Dark blue-gray #12181D
- Cyan #65E1DC
- Mint #A6F5BD
- Amber #F5C76B
- Crimson #990000
- Warm white #EEF7F4
- Muted gray-green #9FB0AB

Every frame in an animation must maintain the same baseline, scale, light
direction, and character proportions. Side-facing motion points right.

Generate on a perfectly flat #FF00FF chroma-key background with no texture,
gradient, floor, cast shadow, reflection, or lighting variation. Never use
#FF00FF inside the assets.

Avoid photorealism, smooth vector lines, blur, painterly texture, 3D
rendering, inconsistent clothing, changing facial features, overlapping
sprites, fake UI, scenery baked behind sprites, and watermarks.
```

## Generated Sheets

1. Student: 8 walking frames, 4 idle frames, and 4 stop/look frames.
2. Flight: 6 airplane frames, 6 motion-trail frames, clouds, globe, pin,
   and route arc.
3. IU milestone: pixel mark, medallion, pennant, gates, arch, and sparkles.
4. Graduation: 6 transformation frames, 4 celebration frames, 6 cap-toss
   frames, diploma, shadow, and sparkles.
5. Portraits: student and graduate portraits plus four timeline icons.
6. Effects: path tiles, travel props, dust, pulse rings, route lines,
   podium, signpost, and skyline silhouettes.
