# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, technical recruiters, and engineering leads evaluating Dhanush
Chandra for Data Engineering roles. They arrive from a job application, LinkedIn,
or a referral, usually skimming on a laptop with limited time. Their job-to-be-done
is fast: "Is this person credible, and are they a fit?" A secondary audience is
fellow engineers gauging technical depth.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes the case for hiring Dhanush as a Data Engineer.
Its differentiator is a built-in AI agent: visitors can ask questions like
"Why should we hire Dhanush?" and get a live, grounded answer drawn from his
background. Success = a recruiter leaves convinced he's worth a conversation,
and ideally has used the AI agent at least once.

## Brand Personality

Precise, credible, engineering-native. Confident without hype. The voice of
someone who ships reliable infrastructure and cares about correctness. Three
words: precise, trustworthy, technical. The interface should evoke the calm
confidence of a well-run data platform, not the noise of a marketing site.

## Anti-references

- An untouched shadcn/ui starter: default indigo primary, plain cards, system
  font. The design must not look like an unmodified template.
- The typical AI-generated portfolio: tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every
  section, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, hero-metric stat blocks.
- Overly flashy / gimmicky: heavy animation, neon, matrix-green "hacker"
  cliche, or motion that undercuts engineering credibility.
- Sterile enterprise-SaaS corporate landing pages.

## Design Principles

1. **Engineered, not decorated.** Every visual choice should read as deliberate
   and built, the way good infrastructure does. No ornament for its own sake.
2. **Credibility over spectacle.** For an engineering hire, trust beats flash.
   Restraint with intent; impact through typography, hierarchy, and one
   confident accent, not through effects.
3. **Show the work.** Prefer real artifacts (a terminal/stack panel, the live AI
   agent) over claims and stock decoration. Demonstrate, don't assert.
4. **Honest content.** No invented metrics, employers, or achievements. The page
   and the AI agent both speak only from real, verifiable background.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1 against its background.
- Meaning is never carried by the ember accent alone; status is always paired
  with text or shape (color-blind safe).
- All motion has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallback (instant, no
  reveal).
- Full keyboard support and visible focus states on every interactive element.
